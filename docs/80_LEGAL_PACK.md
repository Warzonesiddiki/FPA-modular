# 80 — LEGAL PACK (EULA, privacy notice, advisory terms — structure & required content)

> Final wording requires the owner's legal review before commercial distribution; this doc fixes what must exist and the product truths the wording must not contradict. (Not legal advice.)

1. **EULA:** licence grant (per-machine/per-entity per doc 79), restrictions (no reverse engineering beyond law, no resale of licences), ownership of user data (100% user's), warranty disclaimers proportionate to an advisory tool, liability cap, termination + data-export right survival (70 exit guarantee), governing law (owner's jurisdiction — DEC).
2. **Advisory terms (in-app + every export):** the doc 01 §8 disclaimer verbatim; "not an audit/tax/statutory opinion"; human review responsibility.
3. **Privacy notice:** the doc 76 facts in user language: data stays on your machine; no telemetry; AI only if you enable it, aggregates only; support files only when you send them.
4. **Acceptance UX:** first-launch notice screen (plain summary + full text link, "I understand" — not pre-ticked dark patterns), versioned text (re-accept on material change), About screen always shows current version.
5. **Claims discipline:** marketing/user-guide claims must trace to gates (AUD-F truth audit) — no "replaces your finance team" claims; approved positioning is doc 32's sentence.
