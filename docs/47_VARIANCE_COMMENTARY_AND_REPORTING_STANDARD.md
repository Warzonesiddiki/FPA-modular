# 47 — VARIANCE COMMENTARY & MANAGEMENT REPORTING STANDARD

## 1. Commentary structure (human and AI drafts alike; S07 enforces fields)
For each material variance: **What** (line, amounts, % — auto-filled, uneditable numbers) · **Why** (driver/cause in analyst words; categories: volume, price, mix, timing, one-off, rate/FX-n/a) · **Impact** (full-year/forecast effect) · **Action** (owner + date, or "no action — timing"). Exec summary order: headline result → top 3 adverse → top 2 favourable → cash → forecast outlook → asks/risks. Length: exec summary ≤ 220 words; per-variance ≤ 60 words (UI counter; exports truncate never — over-length blocks deck with a hint, analyst edits).
## 2. Wording rules
Numbers always with comparators ("₹12.0L vs budget ₹10.0L, +20%"); no naked percentages; "favourable/adverse" spelled out; no accusatory language (doc 11 ban list applies to humans in UI hints too); one-off vs recurring must be declared for material items; unknown cause is written as "under investigation — owner <name>, by <date>", never invented (an AI draft violating this fails doc 11 guardrails by construction).
## 3. Good vs bad (examples ship in Help S15)
Bad: "Marketing overspent due to various campaigns." Good: "Marketing spent ₹18.4L vs ₹12.0L budget (+53%, adverse): the March product-launch campaign (₹5.1L, one-off, approved by CMO 12-Mar) plus agency retainer increase from April. Full-year impact +₹6.4L; offset by deferring the Q2 brand campaign — owner: Head of Marketing, decision by 20-May."
## 4. Board narrative standard (PPT summary slide)
Result in one line, cash position, 3 decisions/asks max, risks with owners. Anything else goes to appendix — the deck spec (10) enforces slide roles.
